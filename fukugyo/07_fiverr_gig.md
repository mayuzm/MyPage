## Description（1,200文字以内）
Planning Japan for a group is hard. One person wants temples, another wants food, someone hates early mornings, and grandma can't walk all day. And as the planner, you end up doing all the research.

I'm a Japanese local who plans every trip down to the train times. I'll turn everyone's wishes into one clear, minute-by-minute itinerary, so your group can just enjoy Japan.

WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT
- A quick survey for every traveler. I send you a 3-minute form to share with your group, so I plan around everyone's interests, food needs, walking limits and pace, not just yours.
- No guesswork on the day. Each step shows how to get there, how long it takes, and which train you can't miss.
- Ready to share. You get a PDF and an editable Google Doc, plus a Google Maps list, a rainy-day backup and a cost estimate per person.

PERFECT FOR
First-time visitors, families with kids or older parents, and friend groups with different tastes.

PLEASE NOTE
I create the itinerary; I don't make bookings. Opening hours and prices are based on official information at the time of writing.

Message me before ordering if you'd like to check whether your trip is a good fit.

## FAQ
**Can you book hotels, trains or restaurants for me?**
No. I focus on the itinerary itself. I'll mark everything that needs a reservation and when to book it, so you can book with confidence.

**What if some of my group don't answer the survey?**
I'll plan with the answers I have plus what you tell me. If answers haven't come in after 3 days, I'll check in with you.

**We haven't decided which cities to visit. Can you help?**
Yes. Tell me your dates and what your group loves, and I'll suggest a route before building the full plan.

**Do you know about food restrictions in Japan?**
I'll note restaurants with clear options for your group's needs and add tips for ordering. For severe allergies, always confirm directly with the restaurant.

**How should I share the itinerary with my group?**
Share the PDF in your group chat, or invite everyone to the Google Doc. The Maps list works on any phone.

**When should I order?**
Ideally at least 2 weeks before your trip. If it's sooner, message me first.
