## Description（約1,140文字・上限1,200）
Planning Japan for a group is hard. One person wants temples, another wants ramen, someone hates early mornings, and Grandma can't walk all day. And you, the organizer, end up doing all the research.

I'm a Japanese planner living near Tokyo, and I plan every trip with real travel times for every step. I'll turn your whole group's wishes into one clear, step-by-step itinerary, so everyone can just enjoy Japan.

WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT
- A 3-minute survey for every traveler. You just share one link in your group chat. I plan around everyone's interests, pace and walking limits, not only yours.
- No guesswork on the day. Every step shows how to get there, how long it takes, and the departure times that matter.
- Ready to share: a PDF, an editable Google Doc, a Google Maps list, a rainy-day backup and (Standard and up) a cost estimate per person.

PERFECT FOR
First-time visitors, families with kids or older parents, and friend groups with different tastes.

PLEASE NOTE
I create the itinerary; you make the bookings. I'll list what needs booking and by when.

Not sure if your trip fits? Message me first. I usually reply within 24 hours.

## FAQ
**Can you book hotels, trains or restaurants for me?**
No. I focus on the itinerary itself. I'll mark everything that needs a reservation and when to book it, so you can book with confidence.

**What if some of my group don't answer the survey?**
I'll send you a short reminder message to share. If answers haven't come in after 3 days, I'll plan with the answers I have plus what you've told me.

**Will my group's answers be shared with everyone?**
No. Only I see the answers, and I use them just to build the plan.

**We haven't decided which cities to visit. Can you help?**
Yes. Tell me your dates and what your group loves, and I'll suggest a route before building the full plan.

**Can you help with food restrictions?**
The plan doesn't recommend specific restaurants, so please check menus and allergies directly with each place. Your group can easily adjust meals within the plan.

**When should I order?**
Ideally at least 3 weeks before your trip. If it's sooner, message me first.
